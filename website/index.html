How it works

  1. Post a photo

    Your outfit, your desk, your dinner, your trip. Anything you'd happily recommend.

  2. Tap what's in it

    Drop a tag on each thing and say where it's from, or let Locale find the items in the photo for you.

  3. People shop it

    Friends tap a tag to see what it is and where to get it. No more "where's that from?" in your messages.

Made for people who notice things

Where's this from?

Spotted something you can't place? Ask, and people who know will answer. If an answer finds it, they get the credit.

Price drops on your wishlist

Save an item and Locale tells you when it gets cheaper or comes back in stock.

Share from any store

Found it in a store's app? Share it to Locale and the tag fills itself in: name, store and price.

Bring your Instagram

Import the photos you're proudest of, with their original dates, then tag what's in them.

Anyone can earn. You keep 70% of the commission.

When someone buys through an item you tagged, the store pays a commission and 70% of it goes to you. No follower minimum, no application.
