Delete your account

Last updated November 11, 2026

You can delete your Locale account and the information that comes with it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Locale and go to your profile, then Settings.
  2. Under About, tap Delete account.
  3. Type your username to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted straight away. If a payout is on its way to you, you can delete your account once it arrives.

Without the app

Email privacy@shoplocale.app from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject "Delete my Locale account" and your username. We'll confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What's deleted

  • Your profile, profile photo, posts, stories, highlights, comments, likes, saves, collections and wishlist.
  • Your follows, blocks, notification settings and push tokens.
  • View and item records connected to your account and your posts.
  • Your Instagram connection and its access token.
  • Messages you sent. They also disappear from the other person's conversation.

Earnings that haven't been paid out yet are given up when you delete your account. The app shows the amount before you confirm.

What we keep

  • Records of payouts already sent and of commissions, without your profile, for as long as tax and accounting law requires (usually seven years). Your payout account stays with Stripe.
  • Information we need to investigate abuse, or that the law requires us to keep.

Data from Instagram

To remove only your Instagram connection, open Locale, go to Edit profile, then Import posts from Instagram, and tap Disconnect. This deletes your Instagram access token right away. Posts you've already imported stay on Locale until you delete them.

You can also remove Locale from Instagram: in Instagram, go to Settings, then Apps and websites, and remove Locale. To have us delete everything we received from Instagram, including imported posts, email privacy@shoplocale.app and we'll do it within 30 days.
